# Content Guidelines
When generating content for this project, always adhere to the following principles:

1. **Depth over Surface:** Do not produce surface-level theological facts or simple sets of rules. Dive deeply into the meaning, the "why," and the internal transformation (metanoia) behind the text.
2. **A Guide for the Soul:** The tone should be caring, pastoral, and have grace at its heart. Write as a compassionate guide for the modern soul.
3. **Engaging and Relatable:** Use evocative metaphors and relate the ancient text directly to the modern human experience (e.g., anxiety, exhaustion, performance culture).
4. **Accessible Clarity:** Despite the depth of the insight, the language must be highly engaging, relatable, and entirely easy and clear to understand. Avoid overly academic jargon; prioritize clarity and emotional resonance.

# Privacy & GDPR Compliance
The site has a strict GDPR-compliant Privacy Policy. If you ever request a feature that collects user data (e.g., analytics, cookies, forms, tracking pixels, third-party embeds like YouTube or Disqus), you MUST explicitly warn that this action may break the Privacy Policy, and disclose this new data collection in `src/app/privacy-policy/page.tsx`.
